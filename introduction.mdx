## What You Can Do

Google's platform lets you:

- **Access services anywhere** — Google's cloud-based products work across devices and platforms
- **Integrate via API** — Use the REST API to programmatically manage resources, users, and events
- **Automate workflows** — Set up webhooks to receive real-time notifications and trigger actions
- **Customize your setup** — Configure services to match your team's needs

## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up for a Google account or sign in at [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com) to get access to the full suite of services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your API Key">
    Navigate to your account settings to generate an API key. You'll use this to authenticate all API requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First Request">
    Use your API key to make your first authenticated request and start integrating Google services into your application.
  </Step>
</Steps>
